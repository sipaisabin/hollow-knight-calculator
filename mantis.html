💡 Mantis Lords Tips
1
Phase 1 (1 lord) is a tutorial. Learn the sickle timing before Phase 3 arrives.
2
The sickle boomerangs twice — jump once when thrown, again when it returns.
3
Phase 3 (all 3 lords): focus the center lord first to reduce sickle density.
4
Descending Dark can hit all 3 lords if they're close — spike the middle when clustered.
5
Beat them fairly to earn the Mantis village shortcut — extremely useful for exploration.

💡 Traitor Lord Tips
1
The Mantis Claw wall-jump ability is nearly required — the arena has no floor-safe zones in rage mode.
2
His sickle curves more than Mantis Lords' — jump earlier and higher.
3
The infection pools shrink the arena significantly. Kill him fast before you run out of space.
4
Fragile Strength + Quick Slash lets you burst him in his recovery windows before rage mode starts.
5
Equip Isma's Tear — it lets you move through infection without taking damage, changing the fight completely.
