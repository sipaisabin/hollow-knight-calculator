💡 Strategy & Tips
1
Quick Slash is vital — Grimm's attack windows are short. Hit fast and retreat.
2
Learn the fire pillar tell: he extends his hand in the direction the gap will be. Stand there.
3
Against Nightmare King, Stalwart Shell extends your iframes — essential to avoid multi-hit combos.
4
During the bat swarm, stay calm and use tiny movements. Panicking causes mistakes.
5
Fragile Heart gives 2 extra masks — the extra HP has saved many runs in his final phase.
6
Nightmare King becomes significantly more aggressive below half HP. Maintain composure.
