💡 Strategy & Tips
1
Obtain Void Heart — required to trigger this secret ending fight.
2
Quick Slash + Fragile Strength is the premier damage combo — she's vulnerable the entire fight.
3
During the orb phase, dash through orbs diagonally rather than away — they curve.
4
On the platform phase, prioritize staying above the beam over hitting her.
5
Joni's Blessing + Lifeblood Core gives extra buffer masks — very helpful for the final stretch.
6
The opening window after each attack pattern is generous — 3–5 nail hits are usually safe.

Notes:
She is immune during the platform transition animation. You cannot stagger her — she has no stagger state. All phases are a single continuous HP bar at 1200 HP. The fight escalates in aggression as her HP drops below 600.

💡 Strategy & Tips
1
Pure Vessel is the hardest dream boss. Muscle memory is key — practice the patterns repeatedly.
2
His nail combo has an exploitable rhythm: dash through on hit 2, then counter with 1–2 nail hits.
3
Quick Slash + Fragile Strength maximises DPS in his short windows. Every hit counts.
4
Don't panic-heal in Phase 2 — the void pillars punish standing still. Heal only during long recovery animations.
5
Stalwart Shell is nearly mandatory here — his combos come fast and extended iframes save lives.
6
The focus slam has a long charge — use this window aggressively, 3–4 hits are safe.

💡 Strategy & Tips
1
Kill them one at a time. Focus all damage on one knight — never split hits between two.
2
They spawn 2 at a time (2 → 2 → 2). When a pair is down, you get a brief reset window before the next two.
3
Pogo on rolling knights — bouncing off them while they roll deals damage safely and keeps you airborne.
4
Descending Dark is exceptional here — AoE damage hits multiple knights when they cluster.
5
The arena is large — use vertical space. Watcher Knights are bad at tracking aerial movement.
6
Baldur Shell is useful — the 4 free block charges absorb the unexpected hits you get when outnumbered.
